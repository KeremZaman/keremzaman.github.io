**Background.** I'm a [Laz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laz_people) from Turkey. Do you know that [Lazuri (Laz language)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laz_language) is classified as [endangered](https://en.wal.unesco.org/countries/turkiye/languages/laz) by UNESCO?

**Hieroglyphs.** I can read Ancient Egyptian scripts (a.k.a. hieroglyphs).

**Papers & songs.** I keep and occasionally update a [list](https://twitter.com/KeremZaman3/status/1491126566780563456) of NLP papers with their matching songs.

**Academic lineage.** I looked up my academic lineage and found out that [Euler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leonhard_Euler) is my 12th academic great-grandfather, [Jacob Bernoulli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacob_Bernoulli) my 14th, [Leibniz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gottfried_Wilhelm_Leibniz) my 16th, and [Ömer Hayyam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omar_Khayyam) my 35th. As if that weren’t enough, there’s also [Lagrange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph-Louis_Lagrange), [Copernicus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicolaus_Copernicus), and [Ibn Sina (Avicenna)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avicenna).
