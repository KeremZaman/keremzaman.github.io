On the job market

I am on the job market, looking for industry Research Scientist roles in Summer 2027. If you are interested in hiring then please reach out via email.

I’m currently a PhD candidate at the UNC-Chapel Hill where I am advised by Shashank Srivastava. My research lies at the intersection of interpretability and AI safety. I combine black-box and white-box approaches to develop methods for monitoring LLM systems. My broader goal is to build safer and more transparent AI systems by deepening our understanding of how black-box models behave and what drives their behavior.

In summer 2026, I was a Student Researcher at Google DeepMind in New York, working with Neha Kalibhat and Mani Malek. Previously, I interned at the IFM MBZUAI Silicon Valley Lab with Mikhail Yurochkin. Before my PhD, I earned my bachelor’s degree from Boğaziçi University.
